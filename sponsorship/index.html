Sponsorship

Partner With Us

Join leading organizations that collaborate with our group at INSAIT to advance the frontiers of visual AI. Our group brings together researchers working on 3D vision, generative models, multimodal foundation models, embodied agents, and computational imaging. Corporate sponsorship enables us to pursue ambitious research agendas while providing partners with unique access to cutting-edge discoveries and top talent.

Sponsorship benefits

What our partners get

Sponsored research

Fund targeted research projects aligned with your strategic goals, with direct faculty collaboration and joint roadmap planning.

Student internships

Priority access to top graduate students for summer internships and full-time recruitment, with smooth on-boarding into your teams.

Early research access

Pre-publication access to papers, code, and datasets, so your teams can build on our discoveries before public release.

Recruiting

Direct connections to our talented pool of MS, PhD, and postdoc researchers for hiring opportunities across your organization.
