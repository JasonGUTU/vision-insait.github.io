**Research areas:** Computer Vision  
**Google Scholar:** [link](https://scholar.google.com.hk/citations?user=uMQ-G-QAAAAJ&hl=en)

I am a tenure-track faculty member at [INSAIT](https://insait.ai/), in Sofia, Bulgaria, where I lead a group working on visual cognition and intelligence. My research interests include visual perception, visual processing, visual generation, and visual reasoning.

Before joining INSAIT, I received my Ph.D. from the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Sydney in 2024, under the supervision of [Prof. Wanli Ouyang](https://wlouyang.github.io/) and [Prof. Luping Zhou](https://www.sydney.edu.au/engineering/about/our-people/academic-staff/luping-zhou.html). Prior to that, I obtained my Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Engineering from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen [(CUHKSZ)](http://www.cuhk.edu.cn/en) in 2020. I work closely with [Prof. Chao Dong](http://xpixel.group/index.html) and [Prof. Junhua Zhao](http://zhaojunhua.org/). I have also completed internships at the [Multimedia Laboratory](http://mmlab.ie.cuhk.edu.hk/), [SenseTime Research](https://www.sensetime.com/), and [Tencent PCG Applied Research Center](https://arc.tencent.com/en/index).
