We are recruiting **postdoctoral researchers in computer vision** across all directions of the group. We look for excellent candidates with a strong academic background who want to lead world-class research and grow toward independent scientific careers.

## Research areas we work on

We welcome postdoc applications aligned with — but not limited to — the following directions:

- **3D Vision** — reconstruction, neural fields, radiance fields, and geometric reasoning at scale.
- **Egocentric Vision** — first-person video, audio, IMU, and language for situated understanding and forecasting.
- **Robotics** — vision–language–action models, manipulation, sim-to-real transfer, and embodied perception.
- **Space AI** — earth observation, onboard autonomy, and reliable perception for extreme environments.
- **Vision Agent** — multimodal models, tool-using agents, and structured reasoning over images and video.
- **Visual Media** — imaging, restoration, generation, and cinematic quality control for real-world capture.

See [Research directions](/research/) for the latest list and ongoing topics.

## Position at a glance

Postdocs are hosted at **INSAIT** (Sofia University) and work closely with our group's faculty.

- **Eligibility:** PhD in computer science, data science, mathematics, physics, statistics, or electrical engineering. Candidates finishing their PhD may apply, but the degree must be obtained before the start date.
- **Mission:** conduct world-class research in the general field of AI and computer vision, publish at top venues, and mentor PhD and Master's students.
- **Language:** working language is English; international applicants are welcome.

## How to apply

INSAIT runs **rolling admissions** — you may apply at any time. Applications are submitted on the INSAIT platform and require:

- Selected interest areas in computer science and AI
- Research statement
- Curriculum Vitae (CV)
- Bachelor's, Master's, and PhD degree diplomas
- At least two reference letters
- All documents in English, PDF format

Every applicant receives a response within **two months** of applying.

Apply directly via INSAIT's Postdoc page using the button at the top of this page. For full details, see the [INSAIT Postdoc page](https://insait.ai/post-doc-at-insait/).
