**FireScope** is an AI system for high-resolution **wildfire risk mapping** and **interpretable explanations** across Europe. It combines satellite imagery, climate data, and expert risk rasters with a two-stage **reasoning-to-generation** pipeline: a chain-of-thought vision-language **Oracle** estimates regional risk, then an encoder–decoder produces dense risk rasters guided by that prediction.

The public site includes the **Europe 2026** interactive risk viewer, FireScope-Bench on Hugging Face, and links to the paper and code.
