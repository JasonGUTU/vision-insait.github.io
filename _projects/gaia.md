**GAIA** (Geospatial AI and Applications with Foundation Models) is the first international symposium hosted by [INSAIT](https://insait.ai/) and ELLIS Unit Sofia on the intersection of machine learning, foundation models, and geospatial data. It was held in Sofia, Bulgaria, on **24–25 September 2025**, bringing together researchers and practitioners in artificial intelligence, geospatial science, weather and climate, and Earth observation.

The program featured invited talks on remote-sensing geo foundation models, agentic reasoning for Earth observation, physics-inspired models for space science, and applications from space to safety — plus poster sessions, spotlight talks, and networking across academia, industry, and government. GAIA aims to foster interdisciplinary collaboration and catalyze next-generation foundation models tailored for spatial and multimodal applications.
