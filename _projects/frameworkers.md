**FrameWorkers** is a dynamic multi-agent framework for automated AIGC video production. A director-agent plans the workflow and orchestrates a crew of specialist sub-agents — from scripting and storyboarding through generation and editing — into a finished short film from a single prompt.

Instead of a fixed pipeline, a **Director** maintains a task stack and decides what to run next, while an **Assistant** grounds each step in a shared workspace of scripts, assets, and clips. New capabilities can be added by registering sub-agents without redesigning the production flow.
