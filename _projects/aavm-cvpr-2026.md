INSAIT is organizing the **1st Workshop on Agentic AI for Visual Media (AAVM)** at [CVPR 2026](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/), in collaboration with Snap Inc. and Adobe.

The workshop focuses on **agentic AI** for visual media: systems that reason, plan, and execute multi-step creative workflows across production pipelines — beyond single-shot generation. Topics include agentic infrastructures, multimodal generation, visual understanding, multi-agent systems, human–agent collaboration, and evaluation for long-horizon creative tasks.

**Key dates:** paper submission deadline **March 31, 2026**; workshop in **June 2026** (Denver).

**More:** [INSAIT announcement](https://insait.ai/insait-launches-cvpr-2026-workshop-on-agentic-ai-for-visual-media/)
