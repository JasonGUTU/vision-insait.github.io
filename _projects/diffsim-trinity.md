**DiffSim Trinity** is a research line on autonomous driving built on **differentiable simulation** (extending Waymax / Waymo’s differentiable simulator). Learning signals pass through physical dynamics so models learn how actions lead to outcomes — supporting safer, more data-efficient training and counterfactual reasoning.

The trinity covers three settings:

- **Control** — end-to-end policies trained with differentiable simulation on real motion data  
- **Planning** — world models and counterfactual “what-if” prediction under differentiable physics  
- **Search** — online trajectory search using imagined futures at deployment time  

Work from this line appeared at venues including **IROS 2025** and **AAAI 2026**, developed with collaborators at the University of Zurich and ETH Zürich.
