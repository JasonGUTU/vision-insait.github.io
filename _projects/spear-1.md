**SPEAR-1** is a robotic foundation model that enriches non-robotic image data with **3D annotations** and builds on **SPEAR-VLM**, a 3D-aware vision-language model, for language-instructed embodied control. It matches or outperforms models such as π₀-FAST and π₀.5 while using roughly **20× fewer robot demonstrations**.

The public site hosts open weights, 3D-annotated datasets, and the interactive demo.

**Resources:** [Project page](https://spear.insait.ai/) · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.17411)
