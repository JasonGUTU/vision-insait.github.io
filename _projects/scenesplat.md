**SceneSplat** is a generalizable, open-vocabulary encoder that operates **natively on 3D Gaussian Splatting** scenes — fusing geometry and appearance for vision-language pretraining and self-supervised learning without per-scene-only pipelines.

The work introduces **SceneSplat-7K**, a large-scale indoor 3DGS dataset (**7,916 scenes**, **11.27B** Gaussians from seven source benchmarks), with masked Gaussian modeling, self-distillation, and language–Gaussian alignment. The model supports zero-shot semantic segmentation, text-based scene query, and open-vocabulary reasoning over splats.
