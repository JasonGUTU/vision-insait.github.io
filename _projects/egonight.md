![EgoNight teaser](/assets/images/Projects/EgoNight_teaser.png)

EgoNight is the first large-scale benchmark for **nighttime egocentric vision**, built around visual question answering (VQA) with aligned day–night video.

The release includes **EgoNight-VQA** (3,658 QA pairs from 90 videos across 12 question types), real-world captures in Sofia plus synthetic Blender scenes, and auxiliary tasks for depth estimation and day–night retrieval. Experiments show that even strong multimodal models drop sharply when moving from day to night.
